I work on data-driven, robust and deployable AI solutions for healthcare, particularly in low-resource settings. My interests span medical imaging, health informatics and healthcare data science, with current focus on cardiovascular disease (CVD) risk prediction and MRI reconstruction.

Research vision

July 2026 – Present

Project: An Artificial Intelligence Enabled Cardiovascular Disease (CVD) Risk Prediction in Bangladesh.

CVD risk profiling

Developing an AI-enabled framework to predict myocardial infarction and stroke for the Bangladeshi population using routinely available clinical and non-clinical features. Reviewed and compared QRISK3, SCORE2, ASCVD and CVDPoRT for applicability to South Asian populations. Analysed community health data, handling severe class imbalance with SMOTE and stratified cross-validation.
